Eva Santos. Creative director for brands and businesses.

Twenty years leading creative work for brands such as Audi, FC Barcelona, Gillette, P&G and Jägermeister. Former Global Chief Creative Officer of Proximity Worldwide, three times among Forbes' 100 most creative people in business, and winner of more than 250 international awards.

Co-founder of several companies, she knows from the inside what an emerging brand faces. Santa is her creative label, from which she works with ideas, companies and brands at any stage. She also leads the creative direction of UPTAK.
